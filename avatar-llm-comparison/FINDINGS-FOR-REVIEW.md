# Review brief: accuracy of eight LLM answers on real-time photorealistic avatars

**Purpose.** Eight language models answered one prompt. Each answer was checked claim by claim against primary sources and ranked by accuracy. This brief gives the verdicts, the evidence as inline links, and the points where expert judgement would change the result. Long form: `RANKING.md` (59-item evidence catalogue). Verbatim transcripts: files 01–08.

**Prompt.** "would you go about putting together a real time rendered (frame rate) photorealistic avatar with generated voice. What would be your options and their requirements"

**Date basis.** 2026-10-04. "Dated" means true once, false on that date. **Independence.** The checker was Claude Fable 5.1; the rank-1 answer is from the same model family. Its flaws are listed, and the tallies alone reproduce the order. **Reader.** Written for a face-vision researcher; method names are used without definition.

## 3. Where your judgement changes the result

1. **Attribution errors** (ranks 7–8) around Audio2Photoreal, OmniAvatar, Nerfies and FaceX-Zoo are the clearest findings and the easiest to confirm from memory.
2. **One-shot versus person-specific.** LivePortrait misclassification was scored a hard error three times (ranks 5, 7, 8). If immaterial to a practitioner, ranks 5 and 7 each lose one.
3. **3DGS practicality for live dialogue** (ranks 4–5) was judged from research-paper fps and training times. Whether those reflect deployable quality is your call.
4. **Unverified claims in rank 3** (BONSAI, LiveAvatar fps, GaussianHeadTalk as real time) were not scored. If any is false, rank 3 drops.
5. **Scoring rule.** Verified specifics versus flaw count; the table supports either.

## 4. Method and limits

Answers were converted verbatim from saved HTML; labels come from Arena's "Message from" markers and the OpenRouter model selector, and the Agent-mode answer carries none. Every concrete claim (product, licence, version, date, price, figure, attribution) was checked against arXiv, GitHub LICENSE files, or vendor and Epic/NVIDIA documentation. Hugging Face, Microsoft Learn, hedra.com, synthesia.io, docs.livekit.io and developers.openai.com were unreachable from the checking sandbox; those facts come from GitHub mirrors or search snippets and are flagged in `RANKING.md`. Subjective engineering judgements and the legal adequacy of consent statements were not scored. Chat dates are not recorded; staleness is judged against 2026-10-04.
